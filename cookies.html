Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Privacy-First Commitment: CmdVault does not use third-party advertising trackers or commercial surveillance cookies. We use only strictly necessary session tokens for GitHub authentication and local browser storage for your theme preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by web browsers. Modern web applications also use Web Storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) and IndexedDB to store client preferences securely on your device without transmitting unnecessary data on every HTTP request.

2. Technologies Used on CmdVault

Technology Name / Key Purpose Duration
Local Storage cmdvault_theme Stores your dark mode or light mode visual preference so your theme choice persists between visits. Persistent (until cleared)
IndexedDB / Session firebase:authUser:... Stores secure JWT authorization tokens generated by Firebase Auth when you sign in with GitHub, keeping your session active. Session / Persistent until Sign Out
Local Storage cmdvault_saved_commands Locally saves starred and bookmarked terminal commands on your browser without sending them to external databases. Persistent (until cleared)

3. Third-Party Cookies (GitHub & Firebase)

When you click Login with Github, a secure popup window communicates directly with github.com and cmdvalut.firebaseapp.com. These services may set essential security and anti-CSRF cookies necessary to verify the OAuth handshake.

CmdVault does not place or read any third-party marketing, retargeting, or advertising cookies.

4. Managing and Clearing Cookies

You have complete control over cookies and storage:

  • Signing Out: Clicking Sign Out in the top-right user menu immediately clears your active Firebase authentication session tokens from memory.
  • Browser Controls: You can clear all storage and cookies at any time via your browser settings (e.g. Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear browsing data).

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our use of local storage or authentication tokens, please contact us at privacy@cmdvault.dev.
